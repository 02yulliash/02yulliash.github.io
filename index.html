Sales Performance Analysis

Challenge

Two comparable sales periods were available, but there was no reliable way to identify product-level changes, compare performance, or monitor cancellations.

02ECOMMERCE CASE SCENARIO · INDIVIDUAL ANALYSIS · 2026

Northwind Checkout Decline

Transaction and complaint data · Sole analyst · Root cause analysis

Diagnosed a post-redesign sales decline by combining transaction data with the voice of the customer.

  • −38.7% sales decline analyzed
  • −32.3% transaction drop analyzed
  • 53% of complaints linked to checkout
DELIVERABLES

5 Whys · Stakeholder map · RACI · As-Is/To-Be flow · Requirements sample

Open analysis & BA deliverables +

Challenge

A previously stable eCommerce business declined immediately after a redesign, while teams disagreed about whether the cause was marketing, seasonality, or the new experience.

Analysis

Combined quantitative and qualitative evidence, then used 5 Whys, user-role analysis, stakeholder mapping, and a RACI matrix to trace the problem.

Finding & recommendation

Mandatory registration introduced during checkout was the clearest structural cause; the recommendation separated customer data collection from purchase completion.

SAMPLE BA ARTIFACT

Checkout process and requirement set

AS-IS

  1. Cart
  2. Registration required
  3. Account setup
  4. Payment

TO-BE

  1. Cart
  2. Guest or sign in
  3. Payment
  4. Confirmation
Account creation becomes optional after purchase.
BR-01

Customers must be able to complete a purchase without creating an account.

FR-01

The checkout must present a Guest checkout option before registration is requested.

AC-01

Given a customer has items in the cart, when Guest checkout is selected, then the customer can proceed to payment without creating an account.

Portfolio deliverable created from the case scenario; not an implemented production change.

03SAAS ADOPTION SCENARIO · INDEPENDENT FRAMEWORK · 2026

DELIVERABLES

Six-stage journey · Segmentation · KPI framework · Risk signals · Recommendations

Open analysis & BA deliverables +

Challenge

A SaaS customer does not gain value at purchase. Low adoption, limited usage, unclear ownership, and weak engagement can put renewal at risk.

Analysis

Mapped the customer journey, segmented customers, paired usage KPIs with interviews and feedback, and identified friction points by lifecycle stage.
